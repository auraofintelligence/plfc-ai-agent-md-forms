Keep people safe. Keep trust strong.

AI tools are useful helpers, but they should not receive private data, unapproved photos, exact private locations or cultural material without permission.

Five plain rules

These rules are deliberately simple so helpers can remember them before copying anything into AI.

1

Respect Country

Ask, listen and check before sharing cultural material, stories, images or place-based claims.

2

Ask permission

Names, faces, children, private stories and member details need care before use.

3

Keep private data private

Do not paste payment details, contact lists, private Drive links or exact private GPS points into public AI chats.

4

Check facts

AI can sound confident and still be wrong. Check dates, prices, times, links and official wording.

5

Use it well

AI tools are helpers, not the boss. A human still owns the final decision.

OK

Good for all

Use AI to make club work easier, more inclusive and easier to review.

Use privacy lanes

Every generated Markdown file asks for a privacy lane. This helps helpers slow down when a job includes private or permission-needed material.

Private source

Raw member data, payments, exact locations, private media and draft records.

Committee only

Meeting notes, unresolved decisions, action owners and internal drafts.

Public draft

Useful public wording that still needs review.

Public after review

Approved summaries, checked dates, safe photos and confirmed links.

Before publishing, ask these questions

This is a simple human review checklist for AI outputs, captions, scripts, images, videos, song prompts and public pages.

Is it true?

Check dates, times, locations, prices, links, names and official wording.

Is it permitted?

Check faces, children, member details, private messages, media rights and sponsor wording.

Is it respectful?

Check Country, culture, community tone, plain language and whether anyone may be harmed or misrepresented.
