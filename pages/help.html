Common questions

Plain answers for people who are new to AI and Markdown.

Does the form send my answers anywhere?

No. It runs in this browser and creates text you can copy or download.

Can I edit the `.md` file?

Yes. It is plain text. Open it in Notepad, VS Code, a notes app or any text editor.

Can I use it with any AI?

Yes. Paste the file into the AI chat or attach it if the tool allows file uploads.

What should stay out of AI?

Private member data, payment details, private Drive links, exact private GPS, unapproved photos, private committee notes and anything permission-sensitive.
