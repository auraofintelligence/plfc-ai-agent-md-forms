Plan, create, check, share

The old automation plans wanted strong rhythm across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. The safer modern step is to start with one clear content brief.

1

Plan

What are we sharing, who is it for and why now?

2

Create

Ask AI for captions, prompts, short scripts or music ideas.

3

Check

Review facts, names, faces, Country, permissions and sponsor claims.

Different AI tools need different instructions

The form can point each tool in the right direction without making people learn prompt engineering first.

LLM

Chat tools

Summaries, checklists, agendas, captions, emails and plain-English rewrites.

IMG

Image models

Posters, hero images, thumbnails and friendly educational visuals.

VID

Video tools

Short scripts, reels, storyboards, voiceover notes and edit plans.

SONG

Song tools

Jingles, kids learning songs, event chants and playful club music drafts.
